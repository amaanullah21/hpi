# Hafzan Prosperity Index (HPI) Portal

![Field](https://img.shields.io/badge/Field-Macroeconomics-blue.svg)

An interactive analytical platform designed to measure national progress through a human-centered lens. This portal implements the framework proposed in the paper: **"The Hafzan Prosperity Index (HPI): A Multidimensional Human-Centered Framework for National Well-being and Inclusive Growth"** by **Amaanullah Bhatti (Hafzan Osmanoğlu)**.

## 📖 Theoretical Overview

Traditional metrics like GDP often fail to capture the holistic lived experience of a nation's citizens. The **HPI** introduces a new paradigm by integrating ten weighted dimensions that encompass social justice, personal freedom, environmental sustainability, and digital inclusion.

### The 10 Dimensions of HPI
The index operates on a normalized scale (0–100) across ten key domains:
1.  **Economic Equity & Quality**
2.  **Health & Mental Well-being**
3.  **Education & Knowledge Access**
4.  **Environmental Resilience**
5.  **Governance & Civil Liberties**
6.  **Technological Inclusion**
7.  **Social Cohesion & Inclusion**
8.  **Cultural & Intellectual Flourishing**
9.  **Psychological & Temporal Freedom**
10. **Global Shock Resilience**

## 🚀 Key Features

-   **Spider Web Visualization:** Uses a high-fidelity Radar Chart (Chart.js) to map an economy's strengths and weaknesses visually.
-   **Aggregate Scoring:** Real-time calculation of the HPI score based on equal-weight dimension logic.
-   **Benchmarking System:** Professional classification ranges from "Emerging" (< 40) to "Flourishing" (80–100).
-   **Oxford Blue UI:** A professional, academic-grade interface optimized for both research presentation and mobile accessibility.

## 📊 Range Interpretations

| HPI Score | Classification | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **80.0 – 100.0** | 🟢 Flourishing | Exceptional well-being and high equity. |
| **60.0 – 79.9** | 🔵 Developing | Strong foundations with moderate prosperity. |
| **40.0 – 59.9** | 🟠 Stagnant | Basic needs met, but significant structural gaps remain. |
| **< 40.0** | 🔴 Emerging | Critical challenges; urgent policy intervention required. |
